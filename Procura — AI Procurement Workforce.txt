Procura — AI Procurement Workforce

This is one of my favourites.

A company needs to purchase something.

Instead of:

Employee → Procurement → Emails → Suppliers → Quotes → Spreadsheet → Approval → PO

you build:

Request → AI sourcing → comparison → risk analysis → approval → purchase order

Agents:

Request Agent — understands what the employee needs.
Supplier Agent — finds suitable suppliers.
Quote Agent — requests/reads quotations.
Comparison Agent — compares price, quality, terms and delivery.
Risk Agent — checks supplier risk.
Negotiation Agent — prepares negotiation recommendations.
Approval Agent — routes the purchase.
PO Agent — creates the purchase order.
Invoice Agent — later checks invoice against the PO.

The selling point becomes:

"Reduce procurement costs and the amount of manual procurement work."
